PlanDeck Support

PlanDeck is sold on the App Store as PlanDeck Field. It's for viewing, marking up and measuring construction plans on iPhone and iPad.

Common questions

Where are my markups?

On your device, saved automatically. PlanDeck never changes your original files. Your markups stay in PlanDeck. To keep them anywhere else, share or export the plan, or turn on Markup copies. In a plan, Document Information shows the save status.

How do I keep a copy somewhere else?

In Settings → Markup Copies, choose iCloud Drive or Choose Folder. Copies are off until you turn them on. PlanDeck keeps one copy of each marked plan there; choose a different place later, and it moves them. Share and Export work either way.

If I turn copies off, does anything get deleted?

Yes, after asking first: PlanDeck deletes the copies it made, to Recently Deleted where the place has one. Your markups stay in the app. A copy changed in another app is left where it is.

What is Recently Removed?

Plans with markups that you removed from Recent, or whose original file was deleted. To find it, tap ⋯ at the right end of the Recent line, then Recently Removed. Plans stay there for 30 days, then PlanDeck deletes them, with their markups and the copies it made. Until then you can open one to read it, share or export it, restore it, or tap Delete Now. PlanDeck isn't a backup app, so share or export anything you want to keep.

It says iCloud isn't available.

Your markups are safe on your device. Check that you're signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is on in the Settings app, and that you have iCloud storage free. PlanDeck tries again by itself.

It says "Not saved · Retry".

PlanDeck couldn't save your latest markups on this device. Tap it to try again. If your device is low on storage, free some space first. PlanDeck also keeps a recovery file, and offers it back the next time it opens. If this happens on iOS 26.0 with a measurement, updating iOS may help.

The original file of a plan was deleted.

If the plan has no markups, its row turns grey, and tapping it removes it from Recent. If it has markups, it goes to Recently Removed for 30 days, and comes back by itself if the file comes back (for example from Recently Deleted in the Files app). PlanDeck never deletes or changes your original files.

Are my markups in my device backups?

Like any app, PlanDeck's data can be included in your iPhone or iPad backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). Those backups are controlled by you and Apple; PlanDeck has no access to them.

How do I open a plan?

Tap + and choose Open PDFs, or pin a project folder. You can also use Open With from another app.

How do tabs work?

Each plan you open gets its own tab, up to 8. Opening a ninth closes the one you looked at longest ago. To move between open plans, swipe left or right along the bottom bar, or tap the tabs button (the number of open plans) and pick one. Tap × on a tab to close it.

I opened a PDF from Mail or Messages. Where is it?

Open it with Share → PlanDeck. Once you mark it up, the marked-up PDF is in the Files app, in PlanDeck → Imported (in iCloud Drive while Markup copies are on iCloud, otherwise on your device). PlanDeck keeps that file up to date. It deletes it only when you delete the plan from Recently Removed (Delete Now, or after 30 days), and never if you changed the file in another app.

Can I move markups from one plan to another?

Yes. In a plan, tap ⋯ → Save Markups to save its markups as a file in PlanDeck's Saved Markups folder. Those files are yours: PlanDeck never changes or deletes them. In another plan, tap ⋯ → Import Markups, and choose a saved file or another plan with markups. Import only adds to the open plan, and Undo Import takes it back.

Can I print a plan?

Yes. In a plan, tap Share, then Print. iPhone's print screen opens, where you choose the sheets, copies, paper and printer. Your markups print too.

Can I try it without my own plans?

Yes. Download the sample plan (PDF, 26 KB), a fictional house drawn for demonstration. Save it to Files, then in PlanDeck tap + and choose Open PDFs. It's drawn at 1/4" = 1'-0", so measurements come out right.

How accurate are measurements?

They depend on the scale you confirm and on how the PDF was made. Check a known dimension on the sheet first, and confirm anything critical against the dimensions on the plans.
