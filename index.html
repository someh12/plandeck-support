PlanDeck Support

PlanDeck is sold on the App Store as PlanDeck Field. It's for viewing, marking up and measuring construction plans on iPhone and iPad.

Common questions

Where are my markups?

On your device, saved automatically. The original PDF is never changed. In a plan, Document Information shows the save status.

How do I keep a copy somewhere else?

In Settings → Markup Copies, choose iCloud Drive or Choose Folder. Copies are off until you turn them on. Share and Export work either way.

If I turn copies off, does anything get deleted?

No. Copies already made stay where they are.

It says iCloud isn't available.

Your markups are safe on your device. Check that you're signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is on in the Settings app, and that you have iCloud storage free. PlanDeck tries again by itself.

It says "Not saved · Retry".

PlanDeck couldn't save your latest markups on this device. Tap it to try again. If your device is low on storage, free some space first. PlanDeck also keeps a recovery file, and offers it back the next time it opens. If this happens on iOS 26.0 with a measurement, updating iOS may help.

It says the original is "No longer in" a folder.

The PDF you opened was deleted or moved out of that folder. PlanDeck keeps working from its offline copy, and your markups are safe. If the file comes back, the message goes away. To remove the plan, use Remove from Recent in Document Information.

How do I open a plan?

Tap + and choose Open PDFs, or pin a project folder. You can also use Open With from another app.

Can I try it without my own plans?

Yes. Download the sample plan (PDF, 26 KB), a fictional house drawn for demonstration. Save it to Files, then in PlanDeck tap + and choose Open PDFs. It's drawn at 1/4" = 1'-0", so measurements come out right.

How accurate are measurements?

They depend on the scale you confirm and on how the PDF was made. Check a known dimension on the sheet first, and confirm anything critical against the dimensions on the plans.
