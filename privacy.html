PlanDeck Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

PlanDeck is an app for viewing, marking up and measuring construction plans in PDF form. It is published by Sam Ellethy, an individual developer. PlanDeck collects no data. The developer has no servers for this app and cannot see your plans, your markups or anything else you do in it.

What PlanDeck collects

Nothing. PlanDeck has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It contains no third-party code, and no code of its own that sends anything over the internet.

Where your files stay

PlanDeck keeps its offline copies of your plans, your markups and your settings on your device. PlanDeck never changes your original files. Your markups stay in PlanDeck. To keep them anywhere else, share or export the plan.

Your files leave your device only when you make that happen:

  • Markup copies. These are off on a new install unless you turn them on; an install from an earlier version keeps its setting. If you choose iCloud Drive or a folder in Settings, PlanDeck keeps one copy of each marked plan there, and only there. Choose a different place, and PlanDeck moves its copies to it. Copies are handled by Apple's iCloud or by the provider of the folder you chose, under that provider's own terms.
  • Plans you open. If you open or pin a PDF stored with a cloud provider, the system transfers it through that provider, as it would for any app.
  • Share and Export. If you share or export a PDF, it goes where you send it.

Like any app, PlanDeck's data can be included in your iPhone or iPad backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). Those backups are controlled by you and Apple; PlanDeck has no access to them.

Text recognition

When PlanDeck reads text on scanned sheets, for Search or to suggest a scale, it does so on your device with Apple's built-in tools. The results stay on your device, and go when you remove the plan.

Feedback email

If you choose Send Feedback, your own email app opens. PlanDeck attaches nothing to the message. The developer receives only what you choose to send, including your email address, and uses it only to reply.

Third parties

PlanDeck shares no data with anyone, because it collects none. Apple and any file provider you choose handle your files under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it:

  • To remove a plan, swipe left on it in Recent and tap Remove. A plan without markups is removed from PlanDeck at once. Your original file isn't touched. A plan with markups goes to Recently Removed, at the bottom of the Library, for 30 days. Then PlanDeck deletes it, with its markups and the copies PlanDeck made of it. You can delete it sooner there.
  • If a plan's original file is deleted, a plan with markups goes to Recently Removed too, and comes back if the file does.
  • To stop making copies, set Settings → Markup Copies to Off. PlanDeck deletes the copies it made, to Recently Deleted where the place has one. A copy changed in another app is left where it is. Your markups stay in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it keeps on your device.

Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing to request or delete from the developer, apart from any email you sent. To have an email deleted, just ask.

Children

PlanDeck is a work tool and isn't directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

This web page

This page uses no cookies and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep ordinary server logs under its own privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date. If PlanDeck ever began collecting data, this policy and the App Store listing would say so first.
